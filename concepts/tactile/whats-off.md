## Definition

Diagnosing a material means naming what looks wrong, checking the light before the maps and the maps before the scale, and changing one setting at a time, since a broken scene usually has more than one cause.

## Cost lens

Diagnosing costs nothing at run time. The fixes are mostly free settings; the ones that cost are a bigger scan (memory), mipmaps (a third more memory), and an extra shadow or contact shadow (a render of its own).
