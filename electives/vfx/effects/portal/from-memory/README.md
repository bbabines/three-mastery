Build `portal(part,camera)` in `electives/vfx/effects/portal/from-memory/drill.ts`. Return the disc for the viewer to mount and free. The right rack is your visual reference.

## Checklist

- A flat disc sits just above the floor under the selected part and fits its footprint.
- Curl flow bends sample coordinates; curved arms move while the plane stays fixed.
- The center and rim fade softly, and additive energy has no opaque square.
- Changing or clearing selection leaves no old disc behind.

Use three.js docs and source without opening guided steps or `/solutions`. Mark done after matching by eye.
