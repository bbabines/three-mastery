# Environment maps: explain the decision

> **The job:** explain a real 3D decision in five plain sentences.

## Task

A chrome part looks black while the background image looks fine. Explain what the material needs and how you would narrow down the fault. No docs for this one. Write your answer in the box, then reveal the key points and compare. Nothing is graded.

<div data-teach-back>
<ol>
<li>A metal surface mostly reflects its surroundings.</li>
<li>A background picture does not automatically light that surface.</li>
<li>The scene or material needs an environment map for reflections.</li>
<li>Check that the texture loaded and uses a supported format.</li>
<li>Then inspect exposure, roughness, and the actual rendered result.</li>
</ol>
</div>
